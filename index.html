How to survive

You woke chained before the machine. You do not remember owing anything. The Maw remembers for you.

  • Each debt is written on the chalkboard. You get three tolls to pay it.
  • A toll is one purchase on the machine's deck: the VII button buys 7 spins, the III button buys 3 cheaper spins and the dark leaves you a free offering.
  • Pull the lever. Matching symbols in patterns pay coins into your purse (symbol value × pattern multiplier).
  • Hold the Maw (or hold F) to feed it coins. Coins inside the Maw earn interest every time you buy a toll. When it is full, ring the bell.
  • If your tolls are spent and the Maw is still hungry, the ceiling comes down.
  • Omens fill the Dread tube. Full Dread: the Hunger eats half your purse. Three Omens: the spin is void. Three Omens in a line: the Maw devours everything you won this toll.
  • Fever (right tube): every winning spin in a row adds +0.25× to payouts. A dud puts it out.
  • The Peddler's hatch (right wall) sells relics (they sit on the shelf, up to 7) and rites (on the stool, single use, click to perform).
