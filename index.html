How to survive

You woke chained before the machine. You do not remember owing anything. The Maw remembers for you.

  • Each debt is written on the chalkboard. You get three tolls to pay it.
  • A toll is one purchase on the machine's deck: the VII button buys 7 spins, the III button buys 3 cheaper spins and lets you adjust the machine.
  • Whenever you adjust the machine, choose one of three changes: a symbol worth more, more or less often, pattern bonuses, fewer Omens, risky trades and more. The panel on the machine shows every symbol's current value and chance.
  • Pull the lever. Matching symbols in patterns pay coins into your purse (symbol value × pattern multiplier).
  • Hold the Maw (or hold F) to feed it coins. Coins inside the Maw earn interest every time you buy a toll. When it is full, ring the bell.
  • If your tolls are spent and the Maw is still hungry, the ceiling comes down.
  • Omens fill the Dread tube. Full Dread: the Hunger eats half your purse. Three Omens: the spin is void. Three Omens in a line: the Maw devours everything you won this toll.
  • After every settled debt you get a stronger adjustment. Relics come from the Peddler, broken bosses and the voice in the wall.
  • The Jackpot gauge under the arch fills with every spin. When it is full, the next pull is a Jackpot spin.
  • Relics stack: buy the same relic again and its effect grows (two Jars of Moths make Moths four times as common). Copies share one shelf slot and cost more.
  • Click a relic on the shelf or a rite on the stool to inspect it, sell it or perform it. Press H to see all of your relics.
  • Golden spins happen now and then: every pattern pays double.
  • Three or more patterns in one spin grant a free spin (up to four per toll). So do patterns that include a Sand Glass.
  • After a spin you may hold reels: click a reel and it stays put for the next spin. You get one hold per toll.
  • Every third debt is a boss debt with a cruel rule. Break it for a rare relic and a full Jackpot gauge.
  • Sometimes strange events strike a spin. Sometimes eyes light up behind the grate: click it to hear the voice in the wall's deal before they close. Neither is ever quite fair.
  • Some shapes are secret. Find them yourself. Trophies unlock new relics for later descents.
  • Fever (right tube): every winning spin in a row adds +0.25× to payouts. A dud puts it out.
  • The Peddler's hatch (right wall) sells relics (they sit on the shelf, up to 7) and rites (on the stool, single use, click to perform).
