How to survive

You woke chained before the machine. You do not remember owing anything. The Maw remembers for you.

  • Every night you get a number of cranks. Each pull of the lever spins the five reels.
  • Matching symbols in patterns pay coins: symbol value × pattern multiplier.
  • Before the night ends, feed the Maw what it demands. Unused cranks are worth 1 coin each. Coins you keep earn interest.
  • If you run out of cranks and cannot pay, the ceiling comes down. It lowers a little every night either way.
  • Omens fill your Dread. When Dread is full, the Hunger eats half your coins. Three or more Omens in one spin void that spin's winnings.
  • Echo makes symbols copy their neighbours, so patterns become more likely.
  • Fever: every winning spin in a row adds +0.25× to your payouts (up to ×2.5). A spin without a pattern puts it out.
  • Each dusk you choose one of three Offerings: a free relic, a rite, or a bargain (a strong blessing with a curse attached).
  • Relics marked Grows get stronger every time they trigger. Build around them.
  • The Peddler sells relics (permanent, up to 7) and rites (single use, up to 2). Survive ten nights.
