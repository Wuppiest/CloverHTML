# Hollow Toll

A creepy 3D slot-machine roguelike in a single `index.html` (three.js + bloom post-processing, no build step).

You are chained in an undercroft before an old brass machine. Each debt on the chalkboard must be paid within three tolls: press **VII** (7 spins) or **III** (3 cheaper spins + a free offering) on the machine's deck, pull the lever, and feed your winnings to the Maw in the wall. Coins inside the Maw earn interest every toll. Ring the bell to settle. Fail, and the spiked ceiling comes down.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser (internet needed for three.js and fonts from CDN).

**Controls:** click things in the room, or Space pull · 1 / 2 buy a toll · hold F feed the Maw · B ring the bell · E hatch · T turbo · H rules
